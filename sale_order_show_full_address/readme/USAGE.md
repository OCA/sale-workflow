The invoice address and the delivery address are only visible on the sales
order when the user belongs to the *Delivery Address* group, granted by the
*Customer Addresses* setting in *Settings \> Invoicing \> Customer Invoices*.

Open a quotation or a sales order. Under *Invoice Address* and *Delivery
Address* the complete address of the selected contact is shown, and it is
updated as soon as another contact is picked, before saving.
