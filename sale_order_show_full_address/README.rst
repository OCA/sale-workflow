Shows the complete invoice address and delivery address on sales orders,
the same way Odoo already shows the address of the customer.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

The invoice address and the delivery address are only visible on the
sales order when the user belongs to the *Delivery Address* group,
granted by the *Customer Addresses* setting in *Settings > Invoicing >
Customer Invoices*.

Open a quotation or a sales order. Under *Invoice Address* and *Delivery
Address* the complete address of the selected contact is shown, and it
is updated as soon as another contact is picked, before saving.
