Sales Order Effective Delivery Date
-----------------------------------

This module extends the Odoo sales order list and search view with the
existing effective delivery date.

Features
~~~~~~~~

-  Display the effective delivery date in the sales order list, with the
   option to show or hide the column.
-  Search sales orders by effective delivery date.
-  Filter sales orders that have an effective delivery date.
-  Filter sales orders that do not have an effective delivery date.
-  Filter sales orders by a specific effective delivery date or date
   range.
-  Group sales orders by effective delivery date.

**Note:** The module uses the standard Odoo ``effective_date`` field and
does not modify how the date is computed. The effective delivery date is
based on the first completed customer delivery.

Usage
=====

To use the effective delivery date in sales orders:

-  Go to **Sales > Orders > Orders**.
-  The **Effective Delivery Date** can be displayed in the sales order
   list. It can be hidden or shown using the optional column settings.
-  Use the search bar to search for sales orders by effective delivery
   date.
-  Select **Has effective delivery date** to display orders with an
   effective delivery date.
-  Select **Has no effective delivery date** to display orders without
   an effective delivery date.
-  Use **Effective delivery date: Period** to filter sales orders within
   a specific date range.
-  If required, use **Group By > Effective Delivery Date** to group
   sales orders by their effective delivery date.

**Note:** The effective delivery date is calculated by standard Odoo
based on the first completed customer delivery. This module only adds
the existing field to the sales order list and search options; it does
not change the date calculation.
