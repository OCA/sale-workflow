To use this module, you need to:

1. Go to **Contacts** and create a new contact or company.
2. Verify that the **Prospect** field is checked, as the partner has no confirmed sales orders or customer invoices.
3. Go to **Sales** and create a new quotation for the partner.
4. Verify that the partner is still marked as **Prospect** while the quotation is in draft or sent state.
5. Confirm the quotation and verify that the **Prospect** field is unchecked.
6. Cancel the sales order and verify that the **Prospect** field is checked again, provided that the partner has no other confirmed sales orders or customer invoices.

A partner will also stop being considered a prospect when a customer invoice or credit note is created for the partner.

Sales orders and invoices linked to child contacts are also taken into account when determining whether the commercial partner is a prospect.
