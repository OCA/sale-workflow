1. Go to *Sales \> Orders* and create a quotation.
2. In the *Other Info* tab, *Shipping* section, fill in **Delivery
   Address (Free Text)**.
3. Confirm the order: every related transfer shows the address in a
   banner at the top of the form, and prints it on the Delivery Slip and
   Picking Operations reports.
4. If you edit the address after confirming, the transfers reflect the
   change immediately.
