This module is the glue between *Sale Partner Sale Contact* and the
eCommerce (``website_sale``).

When a portal user who is a contact person of a company places an order
on the website, the order is created with the contact person as customer
(``partner_id``). The auto-switch logic of *Sale Partner Sale Contact*
is implemented as an onchange and therefore never runs for website
orders.

This module applies the switch when the website order is confirmed:

- ``partner_id`` is replaced by the commercial partner (the company);
- the contact person who placed the order is stored in the *Sale
  Contact* field (``sale_contact_partner_id``);
- the invoice and delivery addresses chosen during checkout, the fiscal
  position and the payment terms are preserved.

The switch is deliberately not applied while the order is still a cart:
``website_sale`` resets the cart customer to the logged-in user's
partner on every request and looks up abandoned carts by that partner,
so an earlier switch would be reverted and would break cart recovery.

Usage
=====

No specific configuration is needed; the module is installed
automatically when both *Sale Partner Sale Contact* and *eCommerce* are
installed.

1. As a portal user that is a contact person of a company, place an
   order on the website and complete the checkout.
2. Once the order is confirmed, open it in the backend: the *Customer*
   is the company and the *Sale Contact* is the person who placed the
   order.
