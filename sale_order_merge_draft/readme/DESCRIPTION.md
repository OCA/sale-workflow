Merge several quotations of the same customer into a single one.

- The lines of the selected quotations are moved to the oldest one.
- Lines sharing the same product, unit of measure, price, discount and analytic
  distribution are consolidated into one line, with the quantities summed.
- Sections and notes are moved as they are, keeping the order they had on their
  original quotation.
- Source documents and customer references are concatenated on the merged
  quotation.
- The emptied quotations are cancelled, and both sides are cross-referenced in
  the chatter.

This is the sales counterpart of the *Merge RFQs* action of the Purchase
application.
