To use this module, you need to:

- Go to *Sales > Orders > Quotations*.
- Select at least two quotations of the same customer.
- In the *Actions* menu, click on *Merge Quotations*.

Rules applied by the action:

- Quotations are merged only when their company, customer, invoicing address,
  delivery address and currency are identical.
- Each group of at least two such quotations is merged into its oldest one.
- Quotations in another state than *Quotation* or *Quotation Sent* are ignored.
