Merge several quotations of the same customer into a single one.

- The lines of the selected quotations are moved to the oldest one.
- Lines sharing the same product, unit of measure, price, discount and
  analytic distribution are consolidated into one line, with the
  quantities summed.
- Sections and notes are moved as they are, keeping the order they had
  on their original quotation.
- Source documents and customer references are concatenated on the
  merged quotation.
- The emptied quotations are cancelled, and both sides are
  cross-referenced in the chatter.

This is the sales counterpart of the *Merge RFQs* action of the Purchase
application.

Use Cases / Context
===================

- Standard Odoo can already invoice several sale orders together
  (*Create Invoices* with consolidated billing), but cannot produce a
  single order document out of them. This module does.
- The implementation follows ``purchase.order.action_merge()`` of the
  Purchase application: a server action bound to the list view, no
  wizard.
- We did not migrate the `existing module on
  v8.0 <https://github.com/OCA/sale-workflow/tree/8.0/sale_order_merge>`__
  nor the `existing PR to
  v17.0 <https://github.com/OCA/sale-workflow/pull/3904>`__, because it
  was too complex and outdated (merging other states, involving pickings
  and invoices). Instead we implemented a similar approach as today's
  Odoo standard does on the Purchase Order side.

Usage
=====

To use this module, you need to:

- Go to *Sales > Orders > Quotations*.
- Select at least two quotations of the same customer.
- In the *Actions* menu, click on *Merge Quotations*.

Rules applied by the action:

- Quotations are merged only when their company, customer, invoicing
  address, delivery address and currency are identical.
- Each group of at least two such quotations is merged into its oldest
  one.
- Quotations in another state than *Quotation* or *Quotation Sent* are
  ignored.

Development
===========

Two methods are meant to be extended by other modules:

- ``sale.order._prepare_merge_key()`` returns the values two quotations
  must have in common to be merged. Add an element to forbid merging
  quotations that differ on one more field.
- ``sale.order.line._merge_line_key()`` returns the values two lines
  must have in common to be consolidated. Add an element to keep lines
  apart on one more field, or ``self.id`` to keep them from ever being
  consolidated.

Two more hooks are available:

- ``sale.order.line._move_to_order()`` reassigns a line to the merged
  quotation.
- ``sale.order._post_merge_from()`` runs on the merged quotation once
  the lines and the references have been moved over.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- Only quotations in state *Quotation* and *Quotation Sent* can be
  merged. Merging confirmed orders would require moving the related
  deliveries and invoices, which is out of the scope of this module.
- Merged lines keep the price of their original quotation; the pricelist
  of the merged quotation is not re-applied.
- Sections are not deduplicated: two quotations carrying the same
  section end up as two sections. A line consolidated into an existing
  one also leaves its own section behind, which may stay empty.
