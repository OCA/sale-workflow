Glue module between ``sale_order_line_final_price`` and
``sale_multi_discount``.

With ``sale_multi_discount`` installed the line ``discount`` is a
read-only aggregate of ``discount_distribution``, computed by its own
method. The dependency on *Final Price* that
``sale_order_line_final_price`` declares on the standard discount
compute never reaches that field, so typing a final price would leave
the discount untouched.

This module resolves the final price into the discount distribution
instead, through an inverse when the line is written and through an
onchange while it is edited in the form. The unit price coming from the
pricelist stays on the line, as in ``sale_order_line_final_price``, and
the lock of the unit price applies to the discount distribution widget
as well.

Configuration
=============

In *Sales > Configuration > Settings > Pricing*, with *Discounts*
enabled:

-  **Final price discounts** sets what the final price does with the
   discounts of the line:

   -  *Replace the discounts* (default): the distribution becomes the
      single discount that brings the unit price to the final price.
   -  *Append to the pricelist discounts*: the discounts of the
      pricelist rule are kept (e.g. ``40 + 5``) and one more discount is
      appended, the one that brings their result to the final price.

-  **Salespeople Edit Prices and Discounts**:
   ``sale_order_line_final_price`` makes the unit price and the
   discounts read only for anyone who is not a *Sales Administrator*.
   Ticking this option lifts the lock for everybody.

Usage
=====

Fill in *Final Price* on a sale order line: the discount distribution is
worked out at once and the aggregated discount follows. A final price of
80.00 over a unit price of 100.00 gives ``[20]``, or ``[10, 11.11]``
when the pricelist rule already grants 10% and the *append* policy is
active.

The discount is rounded to the *Discount* decimal precision, so the
final price then shows the closest figure the line can really reach:
333.33 over 1,000.00 gives ``[66.67]``, that is 333.30.

Modules built on top can:

-  change the rounding direction of the discount
   (``_get_price_final_discount_rounding_method``);
-  take out of the final price the amounts they fold into the line
   discount (``_get_price_final_reach``);
-  reuse ``_set_discount_to_reach`` to aim the line at any other price.
