1. Log in as a portal user.
2. Navigate to **My Account → Addresses** (`/my/addresses`).
3. Click **Add Address** in the "Delivery address" section and fill in the form.
4. Navigate to **My Quotations** and open a draft or sent quotation.
5. Next to the **Shipping Address**, click **Change** to open the address selector.
6. Select a delivery address and click **Update Shipping Address**.
7. To archive an unused address, click the trash icon on the address card.
