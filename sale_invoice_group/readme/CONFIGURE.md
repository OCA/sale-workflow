To enable the restriction:

- go to *Settings / Sales / Invoicing*
- enable **Restrict Invoicing to a Group** (this is company-specific)

Then choose who may invoice sales orders:

- go to *Settings / Users & Companies / Users*, open the user, and in the
  *Sales* section enable **Invoice Sales Orders**

Sales Administrators and Accounting / Invoicing users keep the capability by
default. While the restriction is disabled, anyone who can edit a sales order
can invoice it, as in standard Odoo.
