1. Go to *Sales \> Configuration \> Sales Teams* and open a team.
2. In the *Territory* tab, select the countries and/or states of the team.
   A country or state can only belong to one team (per company).
3. Set the *Team Leader*: it is used as salesperson for partners assigned to
   the team that have no salesperson yet.
4. Click *Assign Partners* to recompute the team of the existing partners
   located in the territory.
