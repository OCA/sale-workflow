After installing this module:

#. Go to *Sales > Orders > Quotations* or *Sales > Orders > Orders*
#. Click on the **Customer** field
#. Only companies (partners marked as "Is a Company") will be available for selection
#. Individual contacts will not appear in the partner selection dropdown

**Note**: This only restricts the selection of the main partner on sale orders.
Contact persons can still be selected in other fields like delivery addresses
or sale contacts if other modules provide such functionality.
