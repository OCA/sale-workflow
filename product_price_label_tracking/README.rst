This module keeps track of the product labels that must be printed again
after a price change, so shelf labels always show the current price.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Configuration
=============

Go to *Sales > Configuration > Products > Label Tracking Rules* and
create a rule:

- **Responsible**: the user responsible for tracking the price changes.
- **Location**: only follow products with stock on hand in this location
  or any of its children. Goods without stock tracking are ignored,
  unless **Include Non Storable** is set: they are then considered
  always present in the location. Leave empty to follow products
  wherever they are.
- **Pricelist**: follow the price of this pricelist. Leave empty to
  follow the sales price of the product.
- **Products**: a domain on the products to follow. Leave empty to
  follow all goods. Services and combo products are never followed.
- **Scan Every**: how often the rule is scanned. A scheduled action
  checks every hour which rules are due.

The first scan of a rule creates a label track for every product it
follows. If those products are already labelled with the current price,
select their tracks and use **Mark as Printed** instead of printing
them.

Usage
=====

Go to *Sales > Reporting > Label Tracks*. By default, the list shows
your tracks with status *New* or *Updated*: the labels you have to
print.

- *New*: the price changed since the last printed label, or the product
  started matching the rule.
- *Updated*: the price changed again before the label was printed. The
  track shows the latest price.
- *Printed*: the label was printed.
- *Cancelled*: the label does not need printing anymore, for example
  because the price went back to the price on the shelf.

Select tracks and click **Print Labels** to open the label printing
wizard. Remove the lines you don't want to print, choose the format and
print: the tracks are marked as printed. All the selected tracks must
use the same pricelist; group them by pricelist to print one group at a
time.

Use **Mark as Printed** for labels printed another way, **Cancel** to
dismiss a label, and **Reset to New** to print a printed or cancelled
label again.

Use **Scan Now** on a rule to check its prices immediately.
