Go to *Sales > Reporting > Label Tracks*. By default, the list shows your
tracks with status *New* or *Updated*: the labels you have to print.

- *New*: the price changed since the last printed label, or the product
  started matching the rule.
- *Updated*: the price changed again before the label was printed. The
  track shows the latest price.
- *Printed*: the label was printed.
- *Cancelled*: the label does not need printing anymore, for example
  because the price went back to the price on the shelf.

Select tracks and click **Print Labels** to open the label printing
wizard. Remove the lines you don't want to print, choose the format and
print: the tracks are marked as printed. All the selected tracks must use
the same pricelist; group them by pricelist to print one group at a time.

Use **Mark as Printed** for labels printed another way, **Cancel** to
dismiss a label, and **Reset to New** to print a printed or cancelled label
again.

Use **Scan Now** on a rule to check its prices immediately.
