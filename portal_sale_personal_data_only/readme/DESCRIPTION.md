By default, portal users are allowed to see all the sale orders of their
commercial partner. That could cause a leaking of documents between
members and departments of the organization that should stay private.

This module restricts that behaviour so the portal users only see their
own documents: the sale orders whose customer is the user's partner (or
one of its descendants), or the ones the user's partner (or one of its
descendants) is following. The posted invoices linked to those sale
orders are visible as well.
