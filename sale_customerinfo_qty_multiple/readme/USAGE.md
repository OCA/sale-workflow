1. Go to *Sales > Configuration > Prices for customers*, or to the
   *Customers* tab of a product, and open the customer info of a product.
2. Set the **Sale Multiple** (in the unit of measure of the product) and choose
   what to do when a quantity is not a multiple.
3. In a sale order of that customer, type a quantity for the product:
   it is rounded (or highlighted) and a warning tells you what was done.

The *Customer Multiple* column can be shown in the order lines from the
optional columns.
