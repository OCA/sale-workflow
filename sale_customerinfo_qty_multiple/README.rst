Some customers can only be served in multiples of a given quantity of a
product: a full box, a layer or a pallet, or the way their own purchase
system orders it. This module adds a **Sale Multiple** to the customer
info of the product (``product.customerinfo``, the same record that
holds the customer's product code) and applies it to the sale order
lines of that customer:

- **Round down**: 63 units with a multiple of 50 become 50. The customer
  is served the full multiples only, as many distributors do with
  logistic units.
- **Round up**: 63 units become 100.
- **Round to the nearest**: halves go up, so 74 units become 50 and 75
  become 100. It never goes below one multiple: 20 units become 50.
- **Only warn**: the quantity is kept and the line is highlighted.

The rounding is done in the sale order form, with a warning showing the
ordered and the adjusted quantity, and also on lines created or changed
by code (imports, EDI, portals), leaving a note on the order. The
multiple is in the unit of measure of the product; lines in another unit
are converted.

Unlike ``sale_restricted_qty``, which sets the restrictions on the
product for every customer, the multiple here belongs to one customer,
so each customer can have its own (or none).

Usage
=====

1. Go to *Sales > Configuration > Prices for customers*, or to the
   *Customers* tab of a product, and open the customer info of a
   product.
2. Set the **Sale Multiple** (in the unit of measure of the product) and
   choose what to do when a quantity is not a multiple.
3. In a sale order of that customer, type a quantity for the product: it
   is rounded (or highlighted) and a warning tells you what was done.

The *Customer Multiple* column can be shown in the order lines from the
optional columns.
