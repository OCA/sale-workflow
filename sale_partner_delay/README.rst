This module adds a customer-specific delivery lead time field to
partners.

When creating sale order lines, the partner's lead time is automatically
added to the product's delivery lead time to calculate the total
customer lead time.

This is useful when certain customers require additional delivery time
due to:

- Remote locations
- Special delivery requirements
- Customs clearance needs
- Quality control processes
