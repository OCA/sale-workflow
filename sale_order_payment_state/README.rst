This module shows on sales orders whether their invoices are paid.

The standard *Invoice Status* tells how far an order is invoiced, not
whether the invoices are paid. This module adds:

- **Payment Status**: the payment status of the posted customer invoices
  and credit notes of the order: *No Invoice*, *Not Paid*, *Partially
  Paid*, *In Payment* or *Paid*. Draft invoices, and invoices that are
  fully reversed by a credit note, are ignored.
- **Amount Due**: the open amount of those invoices minus the open
  amount of the credit notes, in the order currency. Only visible to
  users with invoicing or accounting read access.

Both fields are stored, so they can be used to filter, group and sum
orders in the list view.

The Payment Status only covers what is invoiced. An order can be *Paid*
while its *Invoice Status* is still *To Invoice*: everything billed so
far is paid, but there is still something left to invoice.

The Payment Status is determined from the remaining invoices as follows:

1. no invoice left: *No Invoice*;
2. all paid: *Paid*;
3. all paid or in payment: *In Payment*;
4. none paid at all: *Not Paid*;
5. otherwise: *Partially Paid*.

Usage
=====

In *Sales > Orders > Orders*:

- The *Payment Status* and *Amount Due* columns are shown next to the
  *Invoice Status*. Both are optional columns.
- Use the filter **Fully Invoiced & Paid** to find orders that are
  invoiced completely, have no draft invoice left and whose invoices are
  all paid: these orders are closed.
- Use the filter **Invoiced but Unpaid** to find orders with posted
  invoices that still have an open amount: these need a follow-up.
- Group by **Payment Status** to get an overview.

The *Payment Status* and *Amount Due* are also shown on the *Other Info*
tab of a confirmed order.
