This module allows to decide how much of the already invoiced down
payment should be deducted from the real next invoice (not down-payment
invoice).

In standard Odoo, when you create a down payment invoice from a sales
order, the down payment amount is fully deducted from the next invoice
created from the sales order.

Now, you can decide whether to deduct the down payments in the invoices
and, in that case, how much to deduct (full or partial, specifying the
amount).

Usage
=====

When invoicing a sales order with down payments, you can choose to
deduct the down payment amount from the total new invoice amount. You
can select to deduct the full down payment amount or specify a partial
amount to deduct (not larger than the total down payment left to
deduct).
