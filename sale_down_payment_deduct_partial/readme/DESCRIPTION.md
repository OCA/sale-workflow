This module allows to decide how much of the already invoiced down payment
should be deducted from the real next invoice (not down-payment invoice).

In standard Odoo, when you create a down payment invoice from a sales order,
the down payment amount is fully deducted from the next invoice created from
the sales order.

Now, you can decide whether to deduct the down payments in the invoices and, in
that case, how much to deduct (full or partial, specifying the amount).
