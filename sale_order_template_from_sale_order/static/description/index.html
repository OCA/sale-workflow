Sale Order Template from Sale Order

This module saves any quotation or sales order as a quotation template. The template keeps sections, notes, product lines and optional products, including quantity, unit of measure, unit price and discount.

Applying that template on a new quotation restores those unit prices and discounts instead of replacing them with the pricelist.

Configuration

Turn on quotation templates once. The Create Template button on a sales order stays hidden until this is done.

  1. Open the Sales app.
  2. Click Configuration, then Settings.
  3. In the Quotations & Orders section, turn on Quotation Templates.
  4. Click Save.

A sales administrator sees Configuration. Settings under it is there for a user who can change system settings. Ask that person when the menu is not on your screen.

Then allow each person who should save an order as a template:

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Click Users & Companies, then Users.
  3. Open that person.
  4. Open the Access Rights tab.
  5. In the Sales section, set Template from Order to Allow.
  6. Click Save.

That person can then open a saved quotation or sales order and use Create Template. If the Sales field above was empty, Allow also sets it to User: Own Documents Only, so they see their own quotations and orders.

On a database installed with demonstration data, the user who signs in as admin already has Allow.

Usage

Open a saved quotation or sales order and click Create Template in the header, immediately after Cancel. The button is shown only to users with Template from Order set to Allow, on every saved order, including quotations, sent quotations, confirmed orders, cancelled orders and locked orders. Type a name and click Create. A notification confirms that the template was created, the wizard closes, and the screen stays on the order. The order itself is not changed.

A sales user can change a quotation template only when they created it, including its lines and optional products. Templates created by someone else stay as they are. Deleting a template stays limited to sales administrators. Any template can still be selected on a quotation.

The new template is listed with the other quotation templates. On a new quotation, select it in Quotation Template. Sections, notes, product lines and optional products are added with the quantity, unit of measure, description, unit price and discount taken from the source order.

Fixed Price is set on every product line and optional product copied from the order, including when the unit price or the discount is zero. While it is set, applying the template uses those stored numbers. Clear Fixed Price to price that line from the pricelist again. A template created by hand, with Fixed Price left off, also uses the pricelist. A stored zero is not treated as “no price” while Fixed Price is on.

The stored unit price is the number from the source line. It is not converted when the new quotation uses another currency or another fiscal position.

On a line created from a fixed price:

  • Changing the quantity keeps the unit price, the discount and the description.
  • Changing the product recomputes the description, the unit price and the discount from that product and the pricelist.
  • Changing only the unit of measure recomputes the unit price and the discount. The description stays.
  • Update Prices recomputes the unit price and the discount from the pricelist.

Duplicating the sales order keeps the fixed unit price, discount and description.

The following lines are not copied:

  • Down payment lines.
  • Combo products. A combo on an order is expanded into component lines, and the template product list does not accept combo products.
  • Component lines of a combo. Their price is a share of the combo, not a standalone price.
