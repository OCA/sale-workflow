Turn on quotation templates once. The **Create Template** button on a sales
order stays hidden until this is done.

1. Open the **Sales** app.
2. Click **Configuration**, then **Settings**.
3. In the **Quotations & Orders** section, turn on **Quotation Templates**.
4. Click **Save**.

A sales administrator sees **Configuration**. **Settings** under it is there
for a user who can change system settings. Ask that person when the menu is
not on your screen.

Then allow each person who should save an order as a template:

1. Open the **Settings** app.
2. Click **Users & Companies**, then **Users**.
3. Open that person.
4. Open the **Access Rights** tab.
5. In the **Sales** section, set **Template from Order** to **Allow**.
6. Click **Save**.

That person can then open a saved quotation or sales order and use
**Create Template**. If the **Sales** field above was empty, **Allow** also
sets it to **User: Own Documents Only**, so they see their own quotations
and orders.

On a database installed with demonstration data, the user who signs in as
**admin** already has **Allow**.
