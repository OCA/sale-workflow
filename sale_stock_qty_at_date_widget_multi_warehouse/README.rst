This module makes the availability icon shown next to the quantity on a
sales order line take into account the stock available on **every
warehouse**, not only the warehouse assigned to the sales order line.

The widget icon next to the quantity is colored as follows:

- **Blue/purple** (the original color): there are enough stock on the
  line's own warehouse to fulfill the demand.
- **Yellow**: the line's own warehouse can not fulfill the demand on its
  own, but the demand can be satisfied by gathering the stock of all the
  warehouses.
- **Red**: there is not enough stock to fulfill the demand even when
  gathering the stock of all the warehouses.

Clicking on the icon opens the availability popover, which now also
shows a breakdown of the available quantity on each warehouse.
